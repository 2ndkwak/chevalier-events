Members and admins can type any label into the "Allergy / dietary tags" field, so near-duplicates and misspellings can build up over time (e.g. "Scallops" and "No Scallops" ending up as two separate tags). Use this screen to fix that.

How this works
  • Rename fixes a typo or wording in place -- everyone who had the old label now has the corrected one, instantly, everywhere it's used.
  • Merge into… folds a duplicate tag into another one you pick -- everyone who had the duplicate now has the target tag instead, and the duplicate is removed.
  • Delete only works on a tag nobody currently has -- otherwise, merge it into the right tag first.
